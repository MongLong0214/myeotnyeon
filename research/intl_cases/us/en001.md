# Laura Owens "Bachelor" fake-pregnancy case — 30 days in jail + 7 years probation

Maricopa County Superior Court (Phoenix, Arizona), state case. First instance. Sentenced 2026-10-23 (Friday). Judge name not stated in the verified sources (do not name). Charges pleaded to: two counts of fraudulent schemes and artifices, one count of perjury, one count of taking the identity of another (initially charged with 14 felonies). Guilty plea via plea deal, August 2026. No trial.

- F1: Owens, 35 (USA Today) / 36 (FOX 10), faked pregnancies and fabricated medical records targeting men, including former "The Bachelor" lead Clayton Echard. (USA Today, FOX 10)
- F2: In the Echard scheme she faked an ultrasound image and a pregnancy video and lied under oath in the civil paternity case she brought against him; schemes ran May 2023 – June 2024. (USA Today)
- F3: In 2023 she sued Echard to establish paternity of claimed unborn twins, later said she miscarried, dropped the claim; the family-court judge referred the matter for criminal investigation. (USA Today)
- F4: Of the four counts, the perjury and identity counts did NOT concern Echard. (USA Today)
- F5: Sentence: 30 days in jail and seven years of probation, plus a deferred 12-month jail term she could serve if she violates probation. (USA Today, FOX 10, KTVU) azfamily: "30 days of flat time plus 12 months of deferred jail starting Oct. 22, 2027"; faces up to 12.5 years in prison if she violates probation (FOX 10/KTVU say "up to 12 years").
- F6: Conditions reported by FOX 10: no contact with victims; mandatory mental-health treatment.
- F7: Prosecutors recommended five years and five months (with 10 years of probation total). (USA Today) Defense / her mother asked for probation only. (azfamily)
- F8: Judge's reasoning: weighed "whether or not she's this sophisticated con person or a very sad individual making some poor choices... So obviously, I think it's the latter." (azfamily) Cited no criminal history, mental health, remorse. (FOX 10)
- F9: Echard in court: "She's not sorry. She's sorry she got caught." (USA Today) After: "She's manipulative and she manipulated the judge." (FOX 10 video)
- F10: Echard's attorney Gregg Woodnick: jail sentence "less than the State and the victims requested, a dangerous person left the court in handcuffs." (USA Today)
