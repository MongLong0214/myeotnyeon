# Aspen "Kartier" Easterling — puppy hit on Twitch livestream — 10 days in jail + 1 year probation

DeKalb County, Georgia (state court; prosecuted by DeKalb County Solicitor-General's Office, prosecutor Samantha Wicker), Judge Alvin Wong. First instance. Plea and sentencing hearing 2026-10-21 (Decaturish: "on Oct. 21 pleaded guilty"; CBS/FOX 5 articles dated Oct. 22–23). Charge: one misdemeanor count of cruelty to animals (second count dropped). Guilty plea; FOX 5 says the sentence was a non-negotiated plea and prosecutors argued for stiffer punishment. No trial.

- F1: Easterling, 19, a rapper and Twitch streamer known as Aspen Kartier, was livestreaming from her Brookhaven, Ga. apartment on Jan. 29, 2026 when she repeatedly struck her 3-month-old Maltipoo puppy (Louie) and lifted it by the skin of its back. (Decaturish, CBS News Atlanta, FOX 5)
- F2: The stream video was played in court. Prosecutor Wicker: "Frankly, I believe the video truly speaks for itself." (Decaturish)
- F3: The puppy appeared healthy after rescue; custody was surrendered and it went to LifeLine Animal Project. (Decaturish, CBS, FOX 5) — non-fatal.
- F4: Sentence: one year total — 10 days in jail with credit for 2 days served, rest on probation; taken to jail from the courtroom; first-offender treatment denied, so the conviction stays on her record. (FOX 5)
- F5: Conditions: 24 anger-management classes, 100 hours community service, animal-cruelty education/prevention program, may not own, possess or be around dogs during probation. (Decaturish, FOX 5)
- F6: Payment for the puppy's care to LifeLine: $3,048 plus $39/month probation fees (FOX 5); $3,348 (Decaturish); "$3,300+" (CBS). Say "about $3,000".
- F7: Judge Wong: "I was going to sentence you to 30 days in jail. I'm not going to do that, but I am going to sentence you to 10 days..." and "This is a very, very symbolic punishment. It won't kill you, but it's not pleasant." (Decaturish)
- F8: Defense (attorney Pat Dixon): she lost her label release, sponsorships and shows; "These 10 minutes don't define who she is." (Decaturish)
