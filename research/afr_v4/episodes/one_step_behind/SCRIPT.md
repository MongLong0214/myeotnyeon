# One Step Behind — original draft, 36 seconds

Not a final-quality sample. This script and its scene modules are an implementation draft awaiting actual rendered review.

## Situation

Late-afternoon courtyard. Lina, a young adult in a rose-coloured coat, pauses in a patch of sun. A reader stands just behind her with an open book. She thinks he is following her. He is trying to keep his page in her shade.

## Screenplay

**S01 — Establish the misunderstanding**
Lina checks the plaza. The reader stands a little behind and to one side, absorbed in his book. Her silhouette casts a long, cool shadow. No information panel or talking presenter.

Narration: “Lina was certain someone was following her.”

**S02 — Suspicion**
Her eyes shift before her head. A short, questioning glance. The reader does not look up. Let the visual relationship be readable before cutting.

Narration: “So she moved.”

**S03 — Follow**
Lina takes one deliberate step. His page leaves the shade. He notices the sunlight and steps back into the shadow after her. Feet plant; the body settles rather than sliding.

Narration: “He moved too.”

**S04 — Repeat with escalation**
She changes direction. A slightly longer look. He follows the shade again without noticing the misunderstanding. The second action must add information, not merely replay the same motion.

Narration: “Again.”

**S05 — Reveal**
Move attention to the open book and the boundary between warm sunlight and cool shade. Lina follows his eyeline down to the page. The audience now knows what he wants.

Narration: “He wasn't following her. He was following her shade.”

**S06 — Payoff**
Her expression softens. She settles where the shade covers the page. He gives a tiny appreciative glance. The ending should feel light, not sentimental or explanatory.

Narration: “Fair enough.”
