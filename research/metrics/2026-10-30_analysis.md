## What the data shows

1. **Every Short gets one feed test of about 1.2k views, then stops.** The last 48 hours brought 1–16 views per video. Distribution never widens.
2. **The cause is the first 1–2 seconds.** Only 36–46% of viewers keep watching (viewed vs swiped). Among those who stay, average view duration is 73–93%, so the content holds once someone stays.
   - Frame 0 is an empty vector phone graphic.
   - Text sits inside a pastel box; the bottom 40% of the frame is empty black.
   - A "형량 퀴즈 #N" branding header comes first, and the narration opens with a slow setup line.
3. **Subscriber conversion is about 0.1%** (7 per 6.15k views). Shorts alone would need about 1M views to reach 1,000.
4. **#4 and #5 never got a feed test** (16 and 21 views). Hypothesis: duplicate suppression after delete and re-upload. Never re-upload.
5. **References** (`format_v4/longform_vs_shorts.md`): the growing Korean channels in this genre are long-form-led (Extol 31k with 9 long-form videos; 제니의 사건기록 20k). Shorts-only channels sit at 189–432 subscribers.
