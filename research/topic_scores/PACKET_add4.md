# Addendum 4 (CEO criteria for political and public-figure topics; takes precedence). Continue.

- **A question form is not an exemption.** Any implication of unconfirmed corruption or influence-peddling ("돈으로 형량을 샀나?") is out, even with a question mark. "유전무죄" (rich-escape) is a selection intent, not an explanation to attach to an individual case.
- **Do not hide the comparison conditions.** Confirm the charges applied, the facts the court accepted, the trial stage and the main sentencing reasons. The demand–sentence gap may be shown, but never assert it as proof of an unjust judgment.
- Mark finality exactly: first instance, appeal or final; suspended, prison or fine; which charges were guilty and which acquitted. Never present a judgment that was later overturned as the final result.
- **Relevance:** only the politician's or chaebol figure's own case. Do not bring in family, staff or associates' personal details.
- Titles, thumbnails and reenactments follow the same boundary: no invented lines, no scenes of money changing hands or backroom deals. The same rules apply to every party.
- Do not generalize sampled comments into public opinion, and do not use outrage intensity as a stand-in for the severity of the crime.
- Each candidate's line must include: **reference date, trial stage, confirmed key facts, and the unconfirmed claims that must not be implied.** Prefer original judgments; if they are inaccessible, cross-check reliable reports. Flag for separate review only the candidates with conflicting facts, or whose major allegations would need treatment outside the judgment.
