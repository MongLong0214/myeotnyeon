# Neck short v4c sources

| Material | Source and use |
|---|---|
| Cervical and upper thoracic vertebrae, skull, mandible, teeth, brain and shoulder bones | BodyParts3D Release 4.0, DBCLS, CC BY 4.0. The v1 and v3 asset records identify the original FMA parts and SHA-256 hashes. Meshes were welded, smoothed, sectioned and re-posed. [DBCLS archive](https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/), [licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| Discs, protrusion, nerve roots, ganglia, cervical curve, landmarks and labels | Procedural assets from the neck episode. V4C uses the v1 C5–C6 disc and adjacent nerve-root sequence. A neutral cervical render introduces the standalone anatomy scene. |
| Person, phone and animation | The v3d character uses MakeHuman/MPFB assets (CC0), custom props and baked Mixamo motion. [MakeHuman asset licence](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/about/license.html) |
| Lighting, typography and audio | Poly Haven Studio Small 09 (CC0), Black Han Sans and Pretendard (OFL 1.1), procedural earshot SFX and local Qwen3-TTS CustomVoice Sohee narration. |

| ID | Claim and evidence |
|---|---|
| C0 | The healthy cervical spine normally has a lordotic curve. Trior Simonovich et al. discuss this alignment and measure the angle of lordosis. [J Spine Surg, 2025](https://jss.amegroups.org/article/view/7375/html) |
| C1 | Line 2 summarizes how cervical loading increases with neck flexion in biomechanical models. The load-arrow graphic illustrates the rise and the narration identifies the result as modelled. [Hansraj, PMID 25393825](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25393825/) · [Barrett et al., 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31594480/) |
| C2 | In lateral radiographs of 34 healthy adults aged 20–39, some segment angles differed during smartphone texting. The single skeleton marks C1–C2 and C3–C4 for significant intervertebral angle differences while standing, and C3–C4 and C4–C5 for significant interdiscal angle differences. The dots locate the segments. [J Spine Surg, 2025](https://jss.amegroups.org/article/view/7375/html) · [PMID 41509843](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41509843/) |
| C3 | Line 6 depicts general cervical disc protrusion toward an exiting nerve root at C5–C6. Root compression can produce radiating upper-limb paresthesia. See Etiology and History and Physical in StatPearls. [Cervical Radiculopathy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK441828/) · [Cervical Disc Herniation](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK546618/) |
| C6 | The posture section shows forward head position and closes with an ear-and-acromion alignment check. |
| C7 | The model starts from a neutral head load of about 5 kg. [Hansraj, PMID 25393825](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25393825/) |
