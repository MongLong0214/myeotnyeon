## 수술편 · Astra draft 2

The opening is a cutaway preview of drilling beside the canal. The chronological operation starts at 3 seconds. The original 44-second Sohee narration and camera cuts at 3, 13 and 33 seconds set the clock for the nine stages. Flap reflection, irrigated bone removal, crown–root separation, separate root delivery, irrigation and closure follow the sequence described in StatPearls. The final clot compresses healing time. ## 수술편 · Astra draft 3

The registered surgical scene carries forward the anatomy, instrument scans, clinical photograph, material maps, clot material and environment light credited under draft 2. The new capillary and stipple atlas, collagen fibers, tooth-section detail and bur marks were made for this episode. The shot-by-shot survey exclusions above also apply to this cut.
Eight local Mantaflow FLIP bakes supply the drilling spray, puncture bead, incision seepage, aspiration, three extraction films and the irrigated socket. The socket uses the measured wall from the scene and a collision floor behind the anatomical cutaway. Saline dilution is shaded across the simulated surface. [Bake source](astra_surgery/draft3/fluid_bakes.py)
The Korean captions use Pretendard Bold from the kit with its white text, amber emphasis and dark outline. The surgery script and fitted voice takes supply the timing. The audio source is the draft 2 AAC stream containing the original narration and eight listen-checked Earshot parts.
