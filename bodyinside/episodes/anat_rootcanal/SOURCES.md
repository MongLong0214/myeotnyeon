# Clinical sources · 신경치료 + 뿌리 끝 고름

The cutaway depicts one restorable permanent lower-left first molar, with two roots and three canals. The mesial root has two independently tracked canals in this example. The visible apical abscess sits beside one mesial apex. The exterior and section geometry depict an illustrative case. [de Pablo et al., systematic review, PMID 21092807](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21092807/)

| C5 | S06 · Endodontic files clean and shape the canals, with irrigation during preparation. | The guideline describes hand and engine-driven preparation, working length, and retaining the path of curved canals. [Peters et al., root canal preparation](https://doi.org/10.1111/aej.12848) · [ESE S3 guideline, instrumentation](https://doi.org/10.1111/iej.13974) |
| C6 | S07 · Irrigant flushes and helps disinfect the root canal system. Drying precedes filling. | Show reflux toward the chamber during the later rinse, using a loose, side-vented tip inside the canal, and a short absorbent-paper drying insert. The visual compresses multiple steps. [Peters et al., irrigation standards](https://doi.org/10.1111/aej.12848) · [Boutsioukis and Arias-Moliz, irrigation review](https://doi.org/10.1111/iej.13739) · [Petschelt, paper-point drying, PMID 2257832](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2257832/) |
| C7 | S08 · Gutta-percha points and sealer fill the prepared canals. | Gutta-percha is the solid core and sealer fills the interface. The ESE includes a single cone with sealer as one supported option. Show one fitted cone per canal, inside each prepared endpoint. [StatPearls, *Endodontic Materials Used To Fill Root Canals*, PMID 36508541](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36508541/) · [ESE S3 guideline, root filling](https://doi.org/10.1111/iej.13974) |
| C8 | S09 · A definitive coronal restoration seals the access. A crown provides cuspal coverage for this substantially damaged molar. | The ESE position statement recommends selecting the restoration based on remaining structure and other case factors. Show a sealed core, then a crown seating on the already prepared tooth at the later restorative visit. [Mannocci et al., ESE position statement, PMID 34378217](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34378217/) · [Full recommendations](https://doi.org/10.1111/iej.13607) |
| C9 | S03–S10 · The clinical steps are compressed across visits. The later visit shows the coronal restoration up close. | Abscess management includes drainage as clinically indicated and control of the source. Depict the endodontic and restorative stages here, with drainage and assessment off-screen. Keep the pocket visible during the explanatory treatment cutaway and frame the later visit above that region. The foreground wipe returns to pre-treatment. [Siqueira and Rôças, treatment review, PMID 23554416](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23554416/) · [ESE S3 guideline, follow-up](https://doi.org/10.1111/iej.13974) |

### Survey models used for visual comparison

Melbourne Dental School’s micro-CT molar provided a check on the irregular occlusal defect and its breach. R-LAB’s longitudinal tooth section provided a comparison for the chamber-to-root course. Artec 3D’s plaster cast supplied an exterior crown and neighboring-tooth reference. MythicaI’s dental set provided a comparison for handpiece and instrument proportions. The production mesh and the working tools retain their registration with the DBCLS jaw across all ten shots. The four reference models carry CC BY 4.0.
