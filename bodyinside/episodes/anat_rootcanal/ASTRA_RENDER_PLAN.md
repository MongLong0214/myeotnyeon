# Astra render plan · 신경치료 + 뿌리 끝 고름

Build one registered M1 and mandible set. Author a fixed section through the tooth and switch between cutaway, prepared and restored states on the same coordinates. Keep the working aperture and apex in the central picture area, with the operative title around y150 at the 540 × 960 caption design size. Animate tools along the modeled canal centerlines. Store the clean plates for the shared caption renderer and assemble with the episode's 30 fps cue clock. [Episode timing](episode.json) · [Asset plan](ASSETS.md)

All estimates are for one local Cycles Metal process with OpenImageDenoise after the current HQ render finishes. Draft is 540 × 960 at 8–12 samples. Final is 1080 × 1920 at 24 adaptive samples as a starting point. Prior wisdom surgery logs recorded about 1.5 s/frame median on the tested 540-wide draft 5 frames, with individual revised frames up to 5.29 s. The estimates below allow for thin moving metal, wet materials and output resolution.
