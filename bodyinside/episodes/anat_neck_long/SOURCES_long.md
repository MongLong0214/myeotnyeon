Method: every paper below was looked up in PubMed E-utilities (esearch/esummary/efetch) on 2026-11-22, and its PMID, DOI and abstract were read. The web pages were fetched the same day and the quoted wording was found in the page text. A claim marked UNVERIFIED is not used in the narration. Claim ids match `episode_long.json` `claims` and the beat `claims` fields. Numbers never appear on screen except "7개" (vertebrae) and "3명 중 1명" (K4). kg values are spoken only, with the model qualifier in the verb.
