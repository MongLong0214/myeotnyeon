# Public assets for Body Inside

Source pages checked on 2026-11-21. **USE** means a commercial rendered video is permitted without mandatory asset credit. **USE-WITH-CREDIT** means the video is permitted with the stated attribution or notices. **EXCLUDE** identifies a restriction, missing download, or unresolved rights. **UNVERIFIED** identifies the particular licence, file, or term I could not confirm. Blender quality estimates come from source descriptions, file listings, and model metadata.

For CC BY credits below, use the asset title, named creator, source URL, applicable [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) or [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) link, and a description of changes where applicable. Put credits in the video description and preserve any supplied copyright, earlier modification notices, or named attribution parties. Suggested wording is provided when a publisher supplies no fixed credit. Where a collection has mixed rights, the verdict applies to the identified items only.

## Production notes

- **Start with geometry that fits the shot.** HRA and VOXEL-MAN cover gross organs and cutaways. Use the arterial plaque mesh, TCIA tumour masks, dental micro-CT and PDB coordinates for episode-specific inserts. For an inguinal hernia scene, the verified canal model provides local anatomy for an authored bulge and sac. Use the distal-radius scan or HumanX acetabulum model for real fracture geometry.
- **Preserve source scale and structure IDs.** Prefer GLB for original materials and multiple objects, OBJ for simple grouped surfaces, and STL for raw geometry. For CT/MRI, extract only the needed labels in Slicer and check orientation and units before smoothing or cutting. Add tissue and skin shading in Cycles.
- **BodyParts3D baseline.** The archive’s current grant is CC BY 4.0 and asks for `BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science licensed under CC Attribution 4.0 International`. The older project information page describes a historical CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan grant. Credit the current archive release and record the actual source used for any derivative.
