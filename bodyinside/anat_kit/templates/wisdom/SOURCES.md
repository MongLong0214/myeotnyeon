# Where a lower wisdom tooth erupts

| ID | Allowed claim | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| C0 | The cheek marker locates the lower jaw on an illustrated adult. | The registered DBCLS molar-to-skin projection on the generic Astra character. The opening introduces this anatomical region. |
| C1 | A wisdom tooth is at the back of the mouth. | NHS patient information, “Wisdom tooth removal”. |
| C2 | The first and second lower molars sit next to each other. | DBCLS 4.0 left lower first and second secondary molars, FMA55704 and FMA55703. |
| C3 | The second molar is behind the first. | Positions of those two meshes in the same unmodified arch. |
| C4 | The eruption site is behind the second molar. | Third-molar position and DBCLS lower gingiva, FMA59764. The dot indicates the region immediately distal to the second molar. |
| C5 | The marked site lies at the back of the tooth row. | Wider camera view of the same coordinates and the labelled neighbouring teeth. |

The DBCLS model supplies the lower jaw, gum and fourteen lower teeth. Its release list has no third-molar mesh. The illustration locates the eruption site relative to the second molar and makes no claim about an unerupted tooth in this individual. The separate sectioning plan requires its own verified tooth geometry before production.
