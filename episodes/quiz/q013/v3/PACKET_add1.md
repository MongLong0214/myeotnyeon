# Addendum (CEO; takes precedence). Continue.
- In the courtroom, do not just shake the camera or chop the shots into 1 s pieces. Make the changes in **statements, judgments and reactions** visible: who says or decides what, and who reacts how.
  - Do not use the scales and gavel effects as repeated decoration.
- A/B/C: readable immediately at the moment of the question, with time to choose (about 2 s or more). On the answer reveal, the chosen option and the sentence must clearly connect.
- Victim: eyes, nose, mouth and a readable expression, but no mocking or exaggerated pain.
