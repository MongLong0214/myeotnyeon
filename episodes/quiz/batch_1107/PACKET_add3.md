**OK:** an action at frame 0, a big question, the #N series marker, the answer at 22 s → next-case teaser and subscribe at 24–25.8 s, the demand–sentence distinction.

**FAIL:**
1. **The 0–3 s hook is weak.** The reenactment fills only about a quarter of the screen. The "fire" is three tiny orange leaf shapes, and the arsonist just stands there.
   - Enlarge the reenactment to at least half the screen. The confirmed act (pouring the accelerant → the fire catching and spreading) must read at a glance.
   - Push in on the action → the fire grows → the passengers' reaction (recoiling, gasping), all within 3 s. No gore. Do not add any unconfirmed action.
2. **3–11 s: the same subway composition held for 8 s**, with only smoke puffs changing. Change the picture every 1.5–2 s:
   - wide → passenger close-up → smoke filling the car → evacuation (confirmed).
3. **12–23 s: a static courtroom for 12 s with a blank "=" card** — the static-panel problem the owner rejected.
   - Remove the blank card.
   - Alternate the judge's expression and the defendant's reaction, with an independent metaphor scene (e.g. the 160 people of the charge shown as figures turning into counters on the scales).
   - Change the picture every 1.5–2 s.
4. **Too many text layers:** top header, two caption lines, small label lines and the series marker.
   - Keep the top header to 1 line of at most 12 characters, captions to 2 lines.
   - Remove the small labels ("구형과 선고 · 시각적 비유", etc.); state reenactment and metaphor through the top-right corner marker only.
5. Title: "…몇 년? 댓글로" is an awkward ending. Use "달리는 지하철에 휘발유 방화…160명 살인미수, 몇 년?" style instead, within the facts.
6. Keep the facts as they are. But in E3, make clear which instance the "검찰 구형 20년" demand comes from (first instance or appeal). If it was a different instance, say so on screen as well (e.g. "1심 구형 20년").
